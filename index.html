The two price columns are nominal dollars for the token scenario set in the «tokens in» / «tokens out» inputs (default 1M in / 250k out). The new column prices the scenario exactly on the official table (input, cached input and output at their own rates) and follows the S(x) slider. The legacy meter emits a single pp read per bracket with no in/out decomposition (the GPU-time rate was never published), so the legacy column applies the cell's measured pp/1M to the scenario's in+out total — hover for the underlying meter read and the per-task prices; a sub-tick weekly read falls back to the session-derived equivalent, marked «session». The verdict chips are the measured per-task comparison of each cell and do not follow the scenario inputs. The meter-native view lives in the threshold chart below. Every verdict shows its margin (the chip's +X %): how much more expensive the loser is, (loser − winner) ÷ winner — it can exceed 100 % when the loser costs more than twice the winner. A verdict exists only when the margin clears the tie band (>2 meter ticks or >5 % of the cheaper cost); inside it the cell is a tie.

Legacy quota vs billed tokens

computed from the raw runs/*.jsonl + batches/*.jsonl lines alone (k=1 cells are the derivatives' baseline; k>1 cells and the calibration/probe workstreams stay out of the cells). The threshold pp/1M prices each cell's OWN measured token mix on the table: an unmeasured cell reports no data and never borrows a threshold. A verdict is {winner, margin_pct}, the margin (loser - winner)/winner: a real margin is >2 meter ticks or >5 % of the cheaper cost; a sub-tick winner (weekly reads 0.0) prices with its session-derived weekly-equivalent, falling back to the loser's own cost (exactly 100 %) with no session reading either; the per-rep quantiles carry the full uncertainty. Both windows ship per bracket: weekly is the primary (the anchor); the session is the secondary signal, session figures are the derived secondary signal, unanchored — never an independent anchor: session $/pp = weekly $/pp / R (R = session:weekly ticks, live verification 6.22, expected range 5-7); the weekly window remains the study's unit of account and its only anchor. Pooled brackets (workload null) are never cells: their per-workload legacy sits in 'pooled' as token-share allocations, marked allocated and never verdicted, and who-wins counts only measured verdicts.
