The slider and the token-quantity input recompute in live JS the new-plan costs, the critical threshold and the verdict margins from the embedded per-cell tokens, rates and anchor — nothing persisted changes (raw immutable; derivatives regenerate only with versioned parameters). Models with a conclusive measured hit-rate keep it (marked «measured S») and the slider cannot move them; models without a published discount are unmoved: S(x) ≡ S0 for them. A custom value that overrides measurements belongs to a stamped re-run (--s), never to this dashboard.

The two price columns are nominal dollars for the number of tokens set in the «tokens» input (default 1M, so the columns read USD per million tokens). The legacy column prices the cell's measured meter read (hover for the underlying pp/1M and the per-task price) at the study's weekly anchor; when the weekly window reads below the tick, the price falls back to the session-derived weekly-equivalent and is marked «session». The new column prices the cell's measured token mix on the official table and follows the S(x) slider (hover for the per-task price). The meter-native view lives in the threshold chart below; the per-task view rides in the tooltips. Every verdict shows its margin (the chip's +X %): how much more expensive the loser is, (loser − winner) ÷ winner — it can exceed 100 % when the loser costs more than twice the winner. A verdict exists only when the margin clears the tie band (>2 meter ticks or >5 % of the cheaper cost); inside it the cell is a tie.
